# FuckGPT Product Brief

## Register

product

## Users

FuckGPT is a self-hosted local operations panel used by one operator to register, manage, export, and re-authorize ChatGPT/Codex accounts.

## Product Purpose

The product keeps account registration, mailbox resources, verification history, desktop handoff, and export workflows in one local web UI. Success means the operator can repeat account workflows without manually reconstructing credentials from logs or ad hoc JSON.

## Brand Personality

Practical, compact, technical. The interface should feel like a dependable local admin tool rather than a public SaaS product.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style landing pages, decorative dashboards, oversized cards, vague onboarding copy, and hiding operational data behind modal-heavy flows.

## Design Principles

- Preserve dense task flow: lists, controls, and status should stay scannable.
- Keep resources explicit: accounts, mailboxes, addresses, and links should be inspectable as separate records.
- Prefer local-first transparency: show JSON-backed paths and plain operational state when useful.
- Make recovery paths obvious: imported or partially linked accounts should show what is missing and how to bind it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use the existing product UI tokens, keyboard-accessible controls, readable contrast in dark and light themes, and no motion that blocks the task.
